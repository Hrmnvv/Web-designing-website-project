Essential Travel Tips

  • Carry a reusable water bottle – Hydration is key in India's climate.
  • Use public transport – It’s both cost-effective and an immersive experience.
  • Respect local customs – Modest clothing and understanding cultural norms will make your trip more enjoyable.
  • Don't miss out on street food – Be sure to try local delicacies, but always from clean vendors.
