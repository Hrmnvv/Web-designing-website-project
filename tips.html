Travel Tips

  • Stay hydrated, especially in hot weather.
  • Use local transport like trains, buses, or tuk-tuks to explore the city.
  • Dress modestly, especially in religious places.
  • Learn a few basic words in Hindi or the local language of the region you’re visiting.
  • Carry a power bank for your phone, as charging points may not always be readily available.
  • Keep your valuables safe in crowded places and beware of pickpockets.
